Delete your BindingTies account

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open BindingTies (the app or the website) and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings and find your family card.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE, and confirm.

It takes effect immediately.

What gets deleted

  • If other parents use the family: your sign-in (email and password or Google link), your private journal entries and their photos, recordings and videos, and your notification settings. The family and everyone else's information stay.
  • If you are the last parent: the whole family is deleted for everyone: calendar, chores and points, medication and emergency information, journals and files, sitter links and invites. Any child logins that remain are left without a family.

Before you delete, you can use Export backup in Settings to keep a copy.

Can't sign in?

Email mcdanieljordan@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my BindingTies account". We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we may keep

Nothing that identifies you stays in the app. Short-lived technical logs and encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are cleared within 30 days. We do not keep the data for any other purpose.

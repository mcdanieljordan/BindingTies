BindingTies Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: November 22, 2026

BindingTies is a family organizer: a shared calendar, reminders, routines, family points, medication reminders, lists, and a place to keep school papers. It is run by Jordan McDaniel for his family and for other families who choose to use it. This page explains in plain language what is stored and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in: your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your name and Google account email. We never see your Google password.
  • What you add to the app: family member names, events, routines, points, medication names and times, lists, and any documents or photos you upload (for example homework or school reports).
  • Device notification details: only if you turn on alerts, so we can send reminders to your device.

Who can see it

Only the people in your family group, and only after a parent or owner of that family approves them. Other families cannot see your data. The app owner does not read your family's content. Service providers that store the data for us (Supabase for the database and file storage, Google for sign-in, and GitHub for hosting the website) process it only to run the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with schools, doctors, or AI services unless you choose to export it and send it yourself.

Children

BindingTies is designed for parents to manage. Children's information is added and controlled by their parents or guardians. Children under 13 should use the app only with a parent's permission and under a parent's account or supervision. A parent can delete a child's information at any time.

Health and school information

Medication reminders and school records are tools to help you stay organized. They are not medical advice, and reminders can fail (for example if a phone is off or has no signal). Always keep a backup alarm for medicine, and follow your doctor's instructions.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete items in the app, leave your family, or ask us to delete your account and all of its data by emailing the contact below. Backups clear within a reasonable time after deletion.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access rules so that only approved members of your family can read it. No online service is perfectly secure, so please avoid storing anything you would not be comfortable keeping in a private cloud account.

Terms of use

  • BindingTies is provided "as is" and may change, have errors, or be unavailable. Use it at your own discretion.
  • Do not use it to store illegal content, or to access another family's information.
  • You are responsible for who you approve into your family and for what you upload.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions or deletion requests: mcdanieljordan@gmail.com.
